02 · What changed

v10 movement → v11 motion clip
Before · v10

Isolated micro-movements

Buoyancy luôn chạy, còn blink, glance và invite được scheduler gọi riêng. Dù có chuyển động, người xem không cảm nhận được một đoạn animation hoàn chỉnh.

  • Random 2.8–5.2 s delay
  • Each gesture was a separate action
  • Very low body travel
  • No narrative arc
Now · v11

One coherent 3.8 s scene

Kael đọc context, quay lại giao tiếp, mời tương tác và kết thúc bằng một concierge nod. Start và end trùng pose nên clip loop liên tục mà không snap.

  • Immediate autoplay
  • Body + eyes + monocle + shadow share one timeline
  • Visible curved travel inside the slot
  • Event clips temporarily take over

05 · Runtime architecture

Simple enough for Codex to rebuild
01 · LOOP

Infinite fixed clip

Seven synchronized WAAPI animations run on one 3800 ms timeline with identical start and end poses.

02 · EVENTS

Computed-pose handoff

Event animations begin and end at current computed transforms, avoiding snaps when they interrupt the loop.

03 · PRIORITY

Direct beats proximity

If a bow is requested during notice, notice accelerates and bow runs next. The model never stacks two conflicting scenes.

04 · LIFECYCLE

Connected means alive

Autoplay starts immediately when mounted. Only a hidden tab, explicit pause, or Reduced Motion can stop the timeline.
