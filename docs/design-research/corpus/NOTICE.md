# Design corpus attribution

The selected CSV corpus under `ui-ux-pro-max/` is derived from [UI UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) at the pinned commit recorded in `manifest.json` and is distributed under the included MIT license.

The direction principles in `governance/design/direction.md` are an adaptation of [Taste Skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) at the pinned commit recorded in `manifest.json`. The upstream `SKILL.md` extract is retained under `taste-skill/` for provenance; its web-specific implementation rules are not copied into the mobile product contract.

Neither upstream repository is a normative product, platform, accessibility, or security authority.
