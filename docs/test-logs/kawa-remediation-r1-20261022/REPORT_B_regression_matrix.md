## What the PASS means, and what it does not

- The 4 flags-off FAIL to PASS rows and the 7 flags-on rows are genuine gating-assertion improvements on the same frozen inputs. No scenario regressed on any gating assertion, with flags off or on.
- The 6 S5 scenarios are **BLOCKED**, as before: J03 (no offer before quote-ready), J14 (payment rail) and J15 (atomicity, RLS, concurrency) need PostgreSQL or more. They were not turned into PASS and nothing was changed to make them move. The audit's observation stands that the six S5 results are one cross-service result counted six times.
- The scenarios use a stubbed model (`{}` for every stage except the turn-intent classifier). A PASS here says the deterministic parts behave; it says nothing about live-model quality.
